# Labour rules (parameters, not legal claims)

Every rule below is a configurable parameter of the planner (`config/planner.yaml`, section `rules`). None of them is legal advice or a statement of what the law, the collective agreement or the store's internal rules require. Each default is marked "confirm": HR, the collective agreement and the works council (CSE) must confirm or change it before any real deployment.

The plan checker (`src/ree/planner/checker/`) enforces exactly these parameters on every roster the solver produces and on every manual edit, independently of the solver. The table is generated by `python -m ree.cli planner labour-rules` and a test keeps this file equal to the code defaults.

| Parameter | Meaning | Default | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `max_daily_hours` | Maximum paid work per day (hours) | 10.0 | General rule, confirm with HR and the collective agreement |
| `min_rest_hours` | Minimum rest between two shifts (hours) | 11.0 | General rule, confirm with HR and the collective agreement |
| `weekly_rest_hours` | Weekly rest, consecutive hours | 35.0 | General rule, confirm with HR and the collective agreement |
| `max_weekly_hours` | Maximum paid work per week (hours) | 48.0 | General rule, confirm with HR and the collective agreement |
| `average_weekly_hours` | Maximum average paid work per week (hours) | 44.0 | General rule, confirm with HR and the collective agreement |
| `average_weeks` | Weeks over which the average is taken | 12 | General rule, confirm with HR and the collective agreement |
| `max_consecutive_days` | Maximum consecutive working days | 6 | Follows from weekly rest, confirm |
| `max_cuts_per_day` | Maximum cuts (split shifts) per day | 1 | Store rule, confirm limits for part-time contracts |
| `cut_minutes` | Default cut length (minutes) | 60 | Store practice, confirm |
| `cut_max_minutes` | Longest cut allowed (minutes) | 90 | Store practice, confirm |
| `hours_tolerance_hours` | Tolerance on weekly paid hours around the contract (hours) | 1.0 | Assumed default, confirm |
| `min_block_hours` | Minimum length of a shift block (hours) | 3.0 | Store practice, confirm |
| `break_minutes_per_hour` | Break minutes per hour worked, paid and counted as work | 3.0 | Store practice, confirm |
| `break_rounding_minutes` | Break length is rounded up to a multiple of (minutes) | 5 | Assumed default, confirm |
| `break_min_block_hours` | Blocks shorter than this have no break (hours) | 3.0 | Assumed default, confirm |
| `break_window_tolerance_minutes` | Break start tolerance around the middle of the block (minutes) | 30 | Assumed default, confirm |
| `max_simultaneous_breaks` | Workers on break at the same time | 2 | Store practice, confirm |
| `limit_case_simultaneous_breaks` | Workers on break at once in a flagged limit case | 3 | Store practice, confirm |
| `max_break_shift_minutes` | Soft limit for moving a break on the day (minutes) | 90 | Store practice, confirm |
| `clock_in_margin_minutes` | Paid minutes before the register start | 10 | Store practice, confirm |

| `history_weeks` | Weeks of burden history used for fairness | 8 | Assumed default, confirm |

## How the checker applies them

- Hours are paid hours: the register window plus 10 minutes before and 5 minutes after each block. Breaks are paid and count as worked time.
- Rest between shifts is measured from the paid end of one day's last block to the paid start of the next day's first block.
- The weekly rest rule asks, for each Sunday to Saturday week, for a gap of at least `weekly_rest_hours` between worked blocks of which a full day (`weekly_rest_hours` minus `min_rest_hours`) lies inside the week. The gap may extend into the neighbouring weeks, and the edges of the roster horizon count as free time because the neighbouring cycles are planned separately.
- The average weekly maximum is checked only over the weeks present in the roster; a 3-week cycle cannot show a 12-week average, so the checker reports it as a warning when fewer than `average_weeks` weeks are available.
- Sunday work is mandatory in this store; the rule only says that it enters the burden rotation and that compensation must be confirmed.
